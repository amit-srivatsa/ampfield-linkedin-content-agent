# Content Pillars

**Last updated:** 2026-09-28

## Pillar: Sustainability

This agent covers exactly one content pillar. All post topics are subtopics within it — no other pillars are in scope right now.

### Subtopics (starting list — expand freely)

1. **Green energy generation** — solar, wind, and how generation mix interacts with storage needs.
2. **Storage solutions** — battery chemistry choices, siting and safety, duration tradeoffs (4-hour vs. 8-hour+).
3. **Grid balancing** — frequency regulation, demand response, how storage displaces or complements peaker plants.
4. **Policy & incentives** — interconnection rules, tax credits, capacity markets — treated factually, not politically.
5. **Project economics** — capex, financing structures, revenue stacking for storage-attached projects.

More subtopics get added here as they come up — this list is a starting frame, not a fixed set.
