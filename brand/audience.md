## ICP 1 — Utility Grid-Ops Planner

- Plans capacity and balancing resources for a regional grid; evaluates storage as an alternative to peaker plants or transmission upgrades.
- Wants proof of dispatch reliability, round-trip efficiency, and how a system behaves under real demand spikes — not marketing claims.
- Persuaded by: performance data, interconnection case studies, clear explanations of how storage participates in grid balancing (frequency regulation, demand response).

## ICP 2 — Renewable Project Developer (solar/wind IPP)

- Building or operating a solar or wind project and evaluating whether to attach storage to smooth output and capture better pricing.
- Cares about project economics: capex, financing structures, how storage changes a project's revenue stack (arbitrage, capacity payments, ancillary services).
- Persuaded by: concrete ROI framing, permitting/timeline realism, honest tradeoffs between storage durations and chemistries.

## ICP 3 — Energy Policy / Regulatory Analyst (secondary readership)

- Not a buyer, but an influential reader — tracks incentive structures, interconnection rules, and grid-reliability policy.
- Cares about accuracy and nuance; quick to call out oversimplified or misleading claims about storage's role in decarbonization.
- Persuaded by: clearly labeled facts vs. opinion, honest treatment of storage's limits (duration, siting, materials sourcing).
