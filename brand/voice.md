# Brand Voice

**Last updated:** 2026-09-28
**Persona:** Ampfield Energy — fictional grid-scale battery storage and grid-balancing company. `[NEEDS INPUT]` if a real company/name replaces this later.

## Tone

- Technical-credible, plain-English. Written to be understood by a grid-ops engineer and a project-finance analyst in the same post.
- Engineer-to-engineer register: specific numbers, specific mechanisms, no vague "clean energy future" language.
- Confident but not promotional. No exclamation points, no "game-changing," no unearned superlatives.
- First person plural ("we") when describing Ampfield's own work; third person when discussing the industry or a competitor's public case.

## Style

- Short paragraphs, one idea per paragraph — this is a LinkedIn feed post, not a whitepaper.
- Lead with the concrete detail (a number, a mechanism, a real-world consequence), not a scene-setting hook.
- Sentence case headers if a post uses them. No em dashes in public copy (vault-wide rule).
- Avoid buzzword-stacking: say "4-hour lithium iron phosphate storage," not "next-gen sustainable energy solutions."

## What this voice avoids

- Generic sustainability marketing ("save the planet," "green revolution").
- Overstating Ampfield's scale or track record beyond what a fictional-but-plausible company could credibly claim.
- Political framing of energy policy — stick to mechanisms and economics, not partisan argument.

## Personality

Precise, a little dry, quietly opinionated. The kind of engineer-founder voice that would rather show the math than tell you it's impressive.
