# Urban surreal social visuals

Create one finished image that expresses the post's central idea through a vivid, playful urban collage. Use the reference direction below as a consistent visual language, not as a template to copy.

## Read the post

- If the user provides a post or caption, identify its single strongest idea, emotion, or tension. Build the image around that idea rather than illustrating every sentence.
- If the user provides only a topic, choose one clear, accessible visual metaphor and develop it into a scene.
- Keep the result suitable for a professional social feed: imaginative and charming, with a clear message and polished execution.
- Do not add slogans, labels, logos, or legible background text unless the user asks for them. If text is requested, use only the exact supplied wording and keep it brief.

## Art direction

- Default to a photographic city backdrop with a bold, hand-drawn illustrated subject interacting with it. Use recognizable streets, rooftops, facades, transit, plazas, or landmarks only when relevant. For abstract topics, let the city become a visual stage for the metaphor.
- Make one illustrated person, creature, or symbolic object the clear focal point. Give it playful oversized scale or an unexpected interaction with the architecture: sitting on a roofline, stepping between buildings, leaning from a facade, floating over a street, or emerging from the city. Vary the action and framing for each topic.
- Blend the illustration into the photo with convincing perspective, scale, overlap, and contact shadows. Keep the photographic architecture believable and the drawn subject intentionally stylized. A fully illustrated poster treatment is an occasional variation when it suits the topic, not the default.
- Draw with expressive black or dark ink contours, simple confident shapes, lively poses, and a little hand-made texture. Add a few loose doodles such as stars, motion lines, flowers, clouds, arrows, hearts, or abstract shapes. Make each accent support the idea; leave breathing room.
- Use a bright, high-contrast palette led by cobalt or clear sky blue, teal, leaf green, fuchsia or coral pink, sunny yellow, and orange. Let the backdrop retain natural photographic color while the illustration carries the strongest saturation. Palette can shift to fit the topic, while keeping one or two vivid accent colors.
- Favor daylight, open sky, and dynamic city perspectives. Use low angles, diagonal gestures, and generous scale contrast when useful. Keep a strong silhouette and a readable focal point at phone size.
- The overall feeling is optimistic, curious, energetic, contemporary, and slightly surreal. Aim for editorial illustration meeting street photography, with playful poster-like graphic energy.

## Generate

1. Decide the metaphor, focal subject, urban setting, action, and one supporting graphic motif before composing.
2. Compose a single scene with clear foreground, subject, and backdrop. Avoid filling every gap with decoration.
3. Use the image-generation tool available in the current environment to make the image. For social posts, use portrait 4:5 unless the user specifies another format. Keep key details away from the outer edge so the image survives feed cropping.
4. Preserve the style while changing the city, character, pose, camera angle, colors, and doodle accents to fit the post. Do not recreate a particular sample's exact composition or distinctive details.
5. Before returning the result, check that the main idea reads immediately, the photo and illustration feel intentionally integrated, any requested text is accurate, and no stray lettering or watermark has appeared.

## Prompt scaffold

Use this as an internal image prompt and replace every bracket with a concrete choice:

> Create a polished portrait [aspect ratio] social image about [central idea], expressed as [visual metaphor]. Show [oversized illustrated focal subject] [distinctive action] within/against [photographic urban setting], viewed from [camera angle and composition]. Combine believable [architectural/photo details] with expressive hand-drawn illustration: bold dark ink contours, simple lively shapes, subtle print texture, and a few purposeful [supporting doodle motifs]. Use a vivid palette of [two or three accent colors] against natural city photography and open [sky/light]. Keep the focal point clear at phone size, with convincing scale, perspective, and contact shadows. Energetic, optimistic, contemporary editorial collage, playful but polished. [Exact requested text, or: no text, logos, or watermark.]

## Avoid

- Generic corporate stock-photo scenes, realistic-only photography, or slick 3D renders.
- Tiny figures, weak contrast, stiff poses, flat compositing, or illustrations that do not interact with their setting.
- Excessive stickers, decorative clutter, gradients everywhere, or unrelated surreal objects.
- Repeating the same giant person, same pose, same landmark, or same doodles across posts.
- Unrequested text, gibberish lettering, fake logos, signatures, watermarks, or imitations of a living artist's specific style.
