## Grid Balancing & Resilience

**Visual Concept**: Dynamic balance, flow, confidence, control

**Urban Backdrop**: Modern city skyline at dawn or dusk / Power distribution center / Smart grid control room city

**Character Setup**:
- Professional figure in balanced, grounded pose (hands up as if conducting, arms spread in balance)
- Color: Navy + hot pink or lime green jacket suggesting energy management
- Expression: Confident, focused, hands-on

**Decorative Elements**:
- Flowing ribbon shapes in electric blue, suggesting current flow
- Stylized electrical nodes or simple circuit-like shapes in lime green
- Stars and motion lines suggesting precision and control
- Balance scales integrated as decorative shapes
- Waves or oscillating lines showing equilibrium

**Color Story**: Blue (stability) + green (growth) + white (clarity) + hot pink (energy)

**Example Prompt**:
> Urban city at dawn with modern skyline. Diverse professional in lime-green jacket with arms outstretched in a balanced, powerful stance. Surrounding elements: flowing electric-blue ribbons, simple star-burst shapes, oscillating wave lines in white and green, a stylized balance-scale decoration. Motion lines suggest energy flow. Bright, optimistic, controlled energy. Modern illustration overlay on cityscape.

## Tips for Best Results

1. **Start with the backdrop**: Choose a location that feels authentic to your message (residential area for community topics, industrial zone for infrastructure, sunny sky for clean energy)

2. **Match character diversity**: Reflect your actual audience or show inclusive representation

3. **Color emphasis**: One color should dominate (hot pink for power/intensity, lime green for growth, sky blue for stability). Use others as accents.

4. **Element count**: 5–8 decorative elements feels right. Too few: sparse. Too many: chaotic.

5. **Tone check**: Each image should feel optimistic and energetic. If it feels heavy or technical, adjust character pose and add more playful elements.

6. **Topic metaphor**: Try to embed one visual metaphor that connects decoration to topic (waves for flow, nodes for connection, growth for expansion, etc.)

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

- ❌ Too many colors with equal weight (should have a clear hierarchy)
- ❌ Character too small or passive (they should dominate and energize)
- ❌ Decorative elements cluttered (5–8, well-distributed)
- ❌ Real photo base oversaturated or too muted (should be slightly desaturated to let illustration pop)
- ❌ Technical jargon visible in illustration (keep metaphorical, not literal)
- ❌ Tone feels corporate or stock-photo (add character personality and playful elements)
- ❌ Missing white outlines (they're essential for separation)
- ❌ Pastel or muted colors (should be bold and saturated)

Remember: **Playful, optimistic, energetic, human-centered.** If it doesn't feel like that, adjust toward more vibrant colors, more dynamic poses, and simpler, joyful decorative choices.
